### ✍️ Reflection

Nike paid $610 million in dividends out of $712 million in earnings, which is a lot to give back when sales are shrinking. Compared with Nvidia giving about half of its cash back to investors, this is a risky move to support its stock price.

The falling revenue means sales are shrinking. Investors care about this more than profit, because profit can be made by raising prices, but sales can't be raised.
