### ✍️ Reflection

SanDisk's stock fell on the news about CXMT even though the two companies don't compete directly, because people fear that other companies like CXMT will enter the market and lower the price of NAND, which SanDisk relies on heavily.

The stock has grown very fast recently, so small events can cause a lot of volatility.
