### ✍️ Reflection

A high mortgage rate means borrowing money for a house costs much more, so fewer people buy or renovate homes. This hurts stores that sell building materials because demand for those materials falls. Essential repairs hold up better than big renovations when money is tight because if people don't buy new houses, they stay in their old ones, so repairs will be needed more.
