### ✍️ Reflection

Amazon and Apple both got good news, but only Amazon grew and not Apple. This might be because the good news for Apple was already priced in, which is also called "buy the rumor, sell the news." Since Apple had already risen about 20% before the report, it was hard for it to rise more when the report came out. The 20% rise happened because investors believed the good report would happen, but when it actually came out, there was no more reason for the stock to go higher.
