### ✍️ Reflection

Stocks went down even though a lot of jobs were added and the economy looked strong, because the Fed is now more likely to raise interest rates. When rates are high, some money leaves the stock market and goes into safer places such as bank deposits.

Even though the economy added 162,000 jobs, the unemployment rate didn't fall, because once jobs are available, more people who don't have a job start to look for one — and they only get counted as unemployed once they start looking. An additional reason is that about 100,000 more people need a job every month, so this also fills up the new jobs.
