### ✍️ Reflection

When oil and gas prices rise, every product's cost rises; when they fall, costs fall too. So the price of oil and gas affects other prices a lot. Inflation is falling but jobs are weak, so the Fed can't raise or lower interest rates. If the Fed raises rates, it will make the weak job market even worse; but if the Fed lowers rates, it will make inflation grow, which they are trying hard to keep down.
