### ✍️ Reflection

The dot plot can move the market more than the hike itself, because the dot plot shows the rates that will come later, sometimes soon and sometimes further away, and that matters more than the one hike happening now.

Credibility matters a lot for the Fed because it can't lie. If they lie often, people will lose trust in the Fed or ask for the people at the Fed to be changed.
