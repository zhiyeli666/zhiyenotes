### ✍️ Reflection

The jobs report may affect the Fed's interest rate decisions. If the jobs report is bad, the Fed might lower interest rates to help people, and when more money is borrowed, some of it goes into the stock market and the market will grow; if the jobs report is good, then the opposite happens. So investors care a lot about the jobs report.
