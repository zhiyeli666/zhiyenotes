### ✍️ Reflection

Companies sell in many countries instead of only their home country because there is limited demand in one country, and selling in many countries increases demand for the product. BYD still grew while Chinese demand was weak, but if this situation lasts longer, and if other countries ban BYD or add tariffs on BYD, then BYD's sales will crash in an instant.
