### ✍️ Reflection

The rising price of memory chips is good news for Micron and bad news for Nvidia, because Nvidia buys memory chips from Micron.

Investors still worry even when the results are good, because other companies can easily make memory chips similar to Micron's, unlike Nvidia's, so prices could fall at any time if another producer enters the market.
