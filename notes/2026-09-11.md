### ✍️ Reflection

Raising interest rates makes the US dollar stronger, which indirectly lowers the price of oil.

The Fed can only lower demand. When the problem comes from supply, the Fed can only change the dollar price of oil, not the actual shortage of oil — and even that effect is short-lived.
