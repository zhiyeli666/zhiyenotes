**Article:** [Disney Raises Streaming Prices Again](https://www.realdaily.com/disney-raises-streaming-prices-again/) — RealDaily

---

### ✍️ Reflection

Raising prices can increase profit directly, but adding subscribers requires advertising or lowering prices, which is harder. Another reason is that Disney already has a lot of subscribers, so adding a significant number of subscribers would not be realistic. The risk of raising prices every year is that Disney might lose subscribers to competition from other streaming companies.
