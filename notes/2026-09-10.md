### ✍️ Reflection

Oracle's revenue and profit both rose while its free cash flow was negative $5 billion, because the AI and cloud part is growing so fast that Oracle spent $28 billion on data centres and GPUs in one quarter, more than the $23 billion its operations brought in. Profit and cash are not the same thing: the cash leaves all at once, but the cost of those data centres is spread over many years on the income statement.

A $664 billion backlog is not the same as $664 billion of revenue, because the work has been sold but not yet delivered. Oracle only counts it as revenue once it provides the service. If Oracle's supply isn't fast enough, even an unlimited backlog would not count.
