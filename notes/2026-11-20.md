### ✍️ Reflection

Its net income fell because rising oil prices increased transportation costs. To exclude the temporary impact of higher oil prices, some investors look at adjusted earnings to see what earnings would have been without unusual events.

Walmart has lower free cash flow because it is spending money on investments such as infrastructure. This doesn't mean it is becoming weaker.
