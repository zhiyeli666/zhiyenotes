### ✍️ Reflection

Nike's sales fell while P&G's grew, because P&G sells things people need every day, while Nike sells relatively high-end shoes that don't have as much demand, since people only replace their shoes once in a while. So when people have less money, they spend less on Nike but still buy P&G products.

Growth from selling more items is healthier than growth from raising prices, because raising prices is only a temporary solution, but selling more items means solid growth.
